name: Petcover manual workflow
description: Product boundary for the Petcover introductory offer.
---

CatStays is the intake and staff follow-up system for the Petcover introductory offer, not an insurance provider. Records may be marked ready to submit, submitted, or active only through explicit staff updates; customer-facing and staff copy must not imply active cover before Petcover confirms it.

**Why:** The cattery needs operator-ready details for manual entry into the Petcover portal, and the existing Revelation Pets integration is unrelated to insurance.

**How to apply:** Keep Petcover application data linked to the booking, customer, and cat; retain declarations and portal/policy references; confirm eligibility and offer wording with Petcover before changing customer-facing claims.
